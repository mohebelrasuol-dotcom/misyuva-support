MİSYUVA Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 1, 2026

MİSYUVA is an internal business management application used by MİSYUVA HALI YIKAMA to manage its daily operations.

Information Used by the App

The application may process business-related information such as customer names, phone numbers, addresses, order information, invoices, pickup and delivery details, payment information, customer notes, and locally stored photos related to orders.

Location Information

Location information may be used to assist with pickup and delivery operations, route calculations, and navigation features.

Third-Party Services

The application may use third-party services such as Firebase and Google Maps to provide application functionality, data storage, synchronization, location services, and related features.

Data Use

Information processed by the application is used only for the operation and management of MİSYUVA HALI YIKAMA and is not sold to third parties.
